Why draft here instead of DocuSign

DocuSign is the incumbent, and it is good at moving a finished PDF through signatures. It does not write the contract for you, and it prices per envelope. CyberSygn starts one step earlier: describe the deal, get a draft, then route it for signature in the same place.
