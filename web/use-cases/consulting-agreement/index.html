Who sends a consulting agreement.

A consulting agreement governs the relationship between a consultant and a client, defining deliverables, IP, and compensation. Pick your line of work to see how the signing flow fits it.

  • Freelancers

    You bill by the project. Every minute spent dragging fields into a contract is unbilled time you could be working on the next gig.

  • Consultants

    Your engagements are high-trust, high-stakes, and frequent. Clients need to sign yesterday and you need an audit trail tomorrow.

  • Coaches

    Onboarding a new client should take five minutes, not fifty. The intake forms, the agreement, the cancellation policy, all on the same device, same session.

  • Real estate agents

    Closings hinge on speed and accuracy. A signature platform that finds every field across a 40-page lease is the difference between a Friday close and a Monday escalation.

  • Agencies

    You sign a hundred contracts a month across clients, contractors, and partners. The detection step alone reclaims days a year.

  • Lawyers

    Engagement letters, NDAs, retainer agreements, every page needs initials, every signature needs an audit certificate. Detection that does the placement makes intake feel like the rest of your practice.

  • Property managers

    You turn units, chase renewals, and sign a lease for every move-in. Every minute spent dragging fields onto a 30-page lease is a minute the unit sits vacant.

  • Accountants

    Your hours are the product, billed to the tenth. Time spent placing signature and date boxes onto an engagement letter is time you can never invoice back.

  • Contractors

    You are on a job site, not behind a desk. Dragging fields onto a work agreement from your phone is unbilled time that should have been a signed change order.

  • Web designers

    You bill for design, not admin. Every minute spent dragging signature and initial boxes onto a project contract is time that never makes it onto the invoice.
