Who sends an employment agreement.

An employment agreement defines the working relationship between an employer and an employee, covering compensation, scope, and confidentiality. Pick your line of work to see how the signing flow fits it.

  • Startups

    Speed is the only moat. Contractor agreements, NDAs, advisor agreements, same templates, every time, signed without friction.

  • Accountants

    Your hours are the product, billed to the tenth. Time spent placing signature and date boxes onto an engagement letter is time you can never invoice back.

  • Recruiters

    You place candidates on a clock, and offers go stale fast. Manually setting up signature fields on every offer letter is unbilled overhead between you and a filled req.
