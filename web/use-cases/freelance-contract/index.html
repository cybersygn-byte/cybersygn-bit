Who sends a freelance contract.

A freelance contract defines scope, deadlines, payment, and revisions between an independent freelancer and a client. Pick your line of work to see how the signing flow fits it.

  • Freelancers

    You bill by the project. Every minute spent dragging fields into a contract is unbilled time you could be working on the next gig.

  • Photographers

    Between shoots, edits, and client management, the last thing you need is a signature tool that fights you. The shoot starts on time when the paperwork is already signed.

  • Web designers

    You bill for design, not admin. Every minute spent dragging signature and initial boxes onto a project contract is time that never makes it onto the invoice.
