Common questions.

How is CyberSygn different from RightSignature?

CyberSygn finds every signature line, initial, date, and checkbox automatically in about 3 seconds. RightSignature makes you drag each box into place by hand. CyberSygn signers click a magic link and sign without creating an account. Same ESIGN Act and UETA compliance; very different time investment.

Is CyberSygn cheaper than RightSignature?

CyberSygn is unlimited at every plan, from $12 (Solo), $19 (Pro, with the AI co-pilot), $29 (Studio, 3 seats), to $79 (Business, white-label + SSO + API). RightSignature starts at $12/mo. Every CyberSygn plan starts with 3 free documents and no card. Origin is $9/month locked for the life of your account for the first 100 founders, and that rate disappears once the cap is filled.

Can I migrate from RightSignature to CyberSygn?

Yes. Cancel your RightSignature subscription, save your templates as PDFs, and upload them to CyberSygn. We detect the fields automatically. Past signed PDFs from RightSignature remain valid signatures; they don't need to be re-signed.
