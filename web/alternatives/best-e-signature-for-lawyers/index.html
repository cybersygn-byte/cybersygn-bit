Common questions.

Questions lawyers ask.

Does CyberSygn produce a record I can keep in the client file?

Yes. Every completed document includes a tamper-evident SHA-256 audit certificate showing who signed and when. You can download and archive it with the matter file.

Do my clients need to create an account to sign a retainer?

No. They open the link and sign in their browser with no login and no app, which removes a common point of friction for new clients.

Are electronic signatures acceptable for engagement letters?

Electronic signatures are widely recognized under laws such as the US ESIGN Act and UETA, and CyberSygn provides a tamper-evident audit trail for each one. We are not a law firm, so apply your own professional judgment to specific documents.
